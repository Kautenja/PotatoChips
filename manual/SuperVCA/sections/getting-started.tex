\manualchapter{Start with a colored VCA}{sec:start}
Super VCA processes audio through Sony S-DSP-style Gaussian interpolation
and signed integer gain. It can soften or reshape a signal while adding
8-bit input quantization. Its frequency control drives the interpolation
engine; it is not a calibrated analog-filter cutoff.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Initialize Super VCA. Patch an oscillator into the left audio input
and connect the left output to a mixer at a low level.
\item Leave Input Gain at 0\,dB, Output Level at 60 and Frequency at its
center setting. Keep Bypass off.
\item Patch a 0--10\,V envelope into the left Level input. Its voltage
multiplies the signed level knob; 0\,V closes the lane.
\item Sweep Frequency and compare the four modes using the top button.
Readjust monitoring level when changing modes.
\end{enumerate}

For a stereo source, connect both audio inputs and outputs. With only the
left input patched, its signal forwards to the right lane so the two sides
can apply different settings to the same source.
