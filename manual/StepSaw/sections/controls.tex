\manualchapter{Control reference}{sec:controls}
Columns run left to right: Pulse 1, Pulse 2, Saw. Each has tuning, FM and
level controls. The pulse columns have duty controls; the saw has SYNC.

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l l X@{}}
\toprule Control & Range; default & Operation \\\midrule
Frequency & $\pm2.5$ oct.; 0 & C4 reference; V/OCT adds octaves. \\
FM depth & $-1$--$+1$; 0 & Adds $aV/10$ octaves, half the depth of many other chip modules. \\
Pulse duty & 0--7; 7 & CV adds one setting per volt, truncated and clipped. \\
Pulse level & 0--15; 12 & Knob times $V/10$, truncated and clipped. \\
Saw level & 0--63; 32 & Knob times $V/10$ controls the saw's accumulator step. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

The eight pulse settings run from 1/16 to 8/16 of a cycle. The frequency
hover display currently uses a different scale from the pitch calculation;
use a tuner for exact settings. The second duty knob may also carry the
first pulse's name in hover help; its position controls Pulse 2.

Pitch, FM and level normal forward with initial values 0, 5 and 10\,V.
Pulse-duty CV normals from Pulse 1 to Pulse 2, initially 0\,V. With no FM
cable, its knob offsets pitch by up to half an octave. Saw SYNC detects rising
edges at 0.02\,V and rearms below 0.01\,V; even a small bipolar audio signal
can reset it. Sync is processed at audio rate.
