\manualchapter{Start with a four-voice bank}{sec:start}
Formerly Blocks. Existing patches and presets remain compatible.

Facets provides four digital oscillators based on the Mutable Instruments
Edges algorithms. Each voice selects a tonal or noise waveform and can have
its own pitch and stepped 8-bit level.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Initialize Facets and connect output 1 to a mixer at a low level.
Use the context menu to select Sine for voice 1.
\item Patch pitch into voice 1 V/OCT. Leave FM at zero and Level at 255.
No gate is required for a continuous tone.
\item Patch a 0--10\,V envelope into voice 1 Level for articulation. Its
level input forwards to later voices until a cable overrides it.
\item Set voices 2--4 to different shapes, tune their frequency knobs,
and listen at output 4 with outputs 1--3 empty to hear the sum.
\end{enumerate}

For a simple bass, use NES Triangle at a low octave, reduce its level and
shape it with the Level input. Sample+Hold and LFSR shapes make rhythmic or
pitched noise as their clock rate changes.
