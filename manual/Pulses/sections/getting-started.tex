\manualchapter{Start with a pulse voice}{sec:start}
Pulses exposes the three tone channels of the Sunsoft FME-7 emulation as a
compact pulse bank. Each has a frequency control, exponential modulation
and a four-bit amplitude control.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Initialize the module. Connect output 1 to a mixer and reduce the
monitoring level. The default Level 10 produces a continuous tone.
\item Patch pitch to the first V/OCT socket and an envelope from 0 to 10\,V
to the first Level socket. Leave FM at zero.
\item Tune the second frequency knob one octave above the first. Pitch and
level forward to the next voice when its matching input is empty.
\item Monitor output 3 with outputs 1 and 2 empty to hear all three voices.
Lower the individual levels to prevent clipping their sum.
\end{enumerate}

The fixed waveform keeps patching simple: use frequency offsets for chords,
close detuning for beating, or audio-rate exponential FM for rougher tones.
