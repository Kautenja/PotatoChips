\manualchapter{Start with tone and noise}{sec:start}
Formerly Mega Tone. Existing patches and presets remain compatible.

Tone 76489 emulates the Texas Instruments SN76489: three square-wave tone
channels and one noise generator. The noise can use a fixed clock or follow
the third tone's period.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Initialize the module and connect Tone 1 output to a mixer at a low
level. Patch a sequencer's pitch to Tone 1 V/OCT.
\item Patch a 0--10\,V envelope to Tone 1 Level. Its default knob value is 7;
raise it for more output, allowing headroom when summing several voices.
\item For a noise-only patch, set all three tone levels to zero and monitor
the final Noise output. Patch a short envelope directly to Noise Level.
\item Leave Noise Period at 0 and sweep Tone 3's frequency. The noise follows
that tone's period even when Tone 3's own Level is zero.
\end{enumerate}
