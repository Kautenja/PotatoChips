\manualchapter{Start with one POKEY voice}{sec:start}
Formerly Pot Keys. Existing patches and presets remain compatible.

Polynomial exposes four Atari POKEY tone/noise channels and selected global
control bits. Clock, noise and high-pass settings interact, making it useful
for coarse digital tones and percussion rather than precision tracking.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Initialize the module. Leave the right-hand global switches off and
connect Tone 1 output to a mixer at a low level.
\item Keep Noise at 7 and Level at 7. Patch pitch to Tone 1 V/OCT and a
0--10\,V envelope to its Level input.
\item Tune with a tuner; the frequency hover label does not match the
calculation's octave scale, and the chip's short period register is coarse.
\item Change Noise one setting at a time. Keep the note and level fixed
while learning how the eight bit combinations change the texture.
\end{enumerate}
