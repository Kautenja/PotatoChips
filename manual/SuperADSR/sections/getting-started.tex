\manualchapter{Start with a gated envelope}{sec:start}
Formerly Super ADSR. Existing patches and presets remain compatible.

Contour runs two Sony S-DSP-style envelopes. Each lane has stepped timing,
a signed amplitude control, and normal/inverted outputs. Its fifth slider
controls sustain decay, despite the legacy panel's RR label.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Initialize the module. Patch a keyboard's 5\,V gate to lane 1 GATE.
\item Connect lane 1 envelope output to an external VCA's CV input. Feed an
oscillator through that VCA, and monitor it at a low level. Attenuate the
envelope if the VCA expects less than roughly 10\,V.
\item Hold a note. Lower Attack for a faster rise; raise it for a slower one.
Keep Amplitude positive while learning the envelope shape.
\item Compare short and long gates. Releasing the gate enters a fixed,
short release; the last slider does not lengthen that release.
\end{enumerate}

For inverted modulation, use the inverted output or set a negative amplitude.
The two outputs of a lane are exact polarity opposites, not separate envelopes.
