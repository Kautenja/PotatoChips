\manualchapter{Start with a gated tone}{sec:start}
Mini Boss exposes one Yamaha YM2612-style operator. Its sine oscillator,
feedback, envelope and internal LFO can form a complete voice or one stage
of an external FM patch. It is one operator per polyphonic channel.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Initialize the module. Connect OUT to a mixer at a low monitoring level.
\item Patch a keyboard or sequencer's pitch to V/OCT and its gate to GATE.
Use a 5\,V gate; without a gate the initialized operator is silent.
\item Leave multiplier at 1, feedback at 0 and TL at 100. The default AR 31
and zero decay rates produce a quick, sustained tone while the gate is high.
\item Lower RR from 15 to lengthen the tail. Raise feedback gradually to
add harmonics, reducing output volume if the result becomes too loud.
\end{enumerate}

For a plucked sound, raise D1R, lower SL, and keep D2R at zero. Adjust the
decay by ear across the pitch range; these rates follow the chip envelope,
not a calibrated time scale.
