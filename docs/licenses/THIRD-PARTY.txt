not every current chip. The unused SCC and TurboGrafx headers were removed
on October 1, 2026. Shared wavetable code is covered conservatively because
its header also credits Shay Green.

does not establish an additional copied-code license from those links;
retain the references and do not treat them as a new permission grant.

-------------------------------------
Files: src/dsp/sony_s_dsp/{adsr,echo,gaussian_interpolation_filter}.hpp
Authors credited locally: Christian Kauten (2020), Shay Green (2006),
Brad Martin (2002).
Local import: 680ac4fa51caf00a57638f09a71497ee9f3f8d45, src/dsp/Spc_Dsp.cpp.
That file explicitly identifies Game_Music_Emu 0.5.2 and Brad Martin's
OpenSPC DSP emulator as its basis. It already states GPL-3.0-or-later.
Upstream family: https://github.com/libgme/game-music-emu/blob/master/gme/Spc_Dsp.cpp
The current upstream file records LGPL-2.1-or-later; include LGPL-2.1.txt
without replacing the local GPL notices. Exact imported upstream revision
and the complete chain from OpenSPC are not recorded.

ADSR, echo, and interpolation are used by enabled modules. The unused full
processor and standalone BRR sample player, their sample-format helpers,
and their tests were removed on October 1, 2026 after removal of the
SuperSynth and SuperSampler Rack modules.

Authors credited locally: Christian Kauten (2020), Shay Green (2006),

Local import: 8131f7dc515daee5710d88e21bdb42464d7c26b0,

Origins: https://www.slack.net/~ant/libs/audio.html
         https://github.com/libgme/game-music-emu
         https://github.com/mamedev/mame

IMPORTANT HISTORICAL DISCREPANCY: the import includes MAME's historical
noncommercial redistribution notice. Its full text is reproduced verbatim
in MAME-HISTORICAL.txt. Current local headers instead state GPL-3.0-or-later.
Modern Game_Music_Emu identifies its MAME engine as GPL-2.0-or-later, but
that alone does not prove the relicensing provenance of this older fork.
The exact upstream revision and evidence covering all retained portions
under the later terms remain unresolved. No GPL-only clearance is asserted
for this family, and this documentation does not change its terms.

The module import a36d88673d66a374f6366d0ff32e4ab3602248c4 explicitly says
"copy CV2612 from TercerBrazo to be modified". Imported src/YM2616.cpp has
Christian Kauten's GPL-3.0-or-later header and evolved into BossFight.cpp;
MiniBoss.cpp was subsequently derived from that module. An upstream URL,
revision, and any additional TercerBrazo notices are not established by
that commit. Preserve this additional lineage rather than attributing the
whole module solely to the emulator authors.

    license. Do not assume project CC terms grant rights in upstream artwork.
  - manual/BossFight/img/envelope.png and manual/MiniBoss/img/envelope.png:
    diagrams associated with the YM2612 envelope explanation; the Boss Fight
    bibliography cites Sega's Genesis Sound Software Manual (1992), but the
    exact image origin and redistribution permission are not recorded.
  - Retired manual/{SuperADSR,SuperEcho,SuperVCA}/img/Chip.png: raster board/chip
    illustrations covered by the project's existing artwork declaration;
    the precise source/export chain is not recorded alongside the PNGs.
  - Outlined type and exports do not by themselves establish the original
    font/design-source permissions. These older font sources remain unknown; the new title font is recorded below.

Keep these gaps visible during the artwork/manual work in specs 003-005/008.

recorded provenance. This inventory does not invent attribution or permission.
