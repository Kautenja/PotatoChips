\manualchapter{Start with an AY tone}{sec:start}
Formerly Jairasullator. Existing patches and presets remain compatible.

Trio AY exposes the General Instrument AY-3-8910's three tone channels,
shared noise generator and shared envelope generator. Each channel can mix
tone and noise and choose fixed or envelope-controlled amplitude.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Initialize the module and connect Pulse A's output to a mixer at a
low level. Keep its Tone on, Noise off and Envelope off.
\item Patch pitch to Pulse A V/OCT. Patch a 0--10\,V external envelope to its
Level input for a straightforward articulated tone; Level defaults to 10.
\item To try the internal envelope, enable Pulse A Envelope. Choose a
repeating mode with the envelope mode button and adjust Envelope Frequency.
\item Turn Noise on for Pulse A and sweep Noise Period to add a shared noise
texture. Leave the other two columns silent or route them independently.
\end{enumerate}

Tone, noise and internal envelope can be combined per column. The internal
envelope's rate and shape are shared by all columns using it, so separate
external Level envelopes are useful when each voice needs its own contour.
