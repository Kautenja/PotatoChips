\manualchapter{Control reference}{sec:controls}
Panel map: \textbf{1} three tone voices: pitch, mix switches, FM and level;
\textbf{2} shared envelope and noise;
\textbf{3} sync inputs and voice outputs.

The three tone columns contain frequency, FM, level and Tone/Noise/Envelope
switches. The shared controls set envelope frequency and shape, plus noise
period. Sync and CV sockets sit below the corresponding controls.

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l l X@{}}
\toprule Control & Range; default & Operation \\\midrule
Tone frequency & $\pm5$ oct.; 0 & C4 reference; V/OCT adds octaves. \\
Tone FM & $-1$--$+1$; 0 & Adds $aV/5$ octaves. \\
Level & 0--15; 10 & In ordinary tone mode, knob times $V/10$, rounded/clipped. \\
Noise period & 0--31; 0 & Adds $31V/7$, floors/clamps, then reverses the period register. \\
Envelope frequency & $-5.5$--9; 1.75 & $2^{p+V}$\,Hz before chip quantization; default about 3.36\,Hz. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

Tone, Noise and Envelope CV toggle their respective panel states while high
(2\,V high, 0.01\,V low). Tone defaults on; Noise and Envelope default off.
Pitch, FM and Level normal across A, B and C, starting at 0, 5 and 10\,V.
Envelope V/OCT inherits Pulse C pitch when empty. The configured envelope
FM parameter is not applied by the current processing path.

Sync detects rising edges at 2\,V and rearms below 0.01\,V. Pulse sync forwards
A to B to C, then to envelope sync. Patch Pulse A Sync explicitly when you
need a known reset source: its empty-input fallback currently reads the
preceding envelope-mode input instead of a fixed zero.
