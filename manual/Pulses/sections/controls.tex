\manualchapter{Control reference}{sec:controls}
The three columns repeat frequency, bipolar FM depth and level, followed by
V/OCT, FM, Level and output sockets. There is no duty-cycle, noise or sync
control on this module.

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l l X@{}}
\toprule Control & Range; default & Operation \\\midrule
Frequency & $\pm4.5$ oct.; 0 & Reference C4; V/OCT adds one octave per volt. \\
FM depth & $-1$--$+1$; 0 & Adds $aV/5$ octaves to tuning. \\
Level & 0--15; 10 & Knob times $V/10$, rounded and clamped to 0--15. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

V/OCT, FM and Level normal forward independently from column to column.
The first sockets normal to 0, 5 and 10\,V, respectively. An empty FM socket
therefore makes its depth knob a fine-tuning control. An inserted cable
replaces the normal from that point onward. Negative Level CV silences the
voice; it does not invert it.

The chip's integer frequency register limits the extremes of tuning.
At high pitches, nearby CV values can select the same register value.
Level has sixteen states, so slow amplitude ramps can reveal steps.
