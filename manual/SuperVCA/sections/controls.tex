The top mode button is shared by both lanes. Left and right columns contain
Frequency at the top, Output Level in the middle and Input Gain above the
audio inputs at the bottom, with the corresponding CV sockets.
Bypass switches both lanes together.

Audio, frequency CV and level CV normal from left to right, starting
at 0, 0 and 10\,V respectively. Patch a right-hand socket to override its
left-hand counterpart. Outputs remain separate rather than summing together.

Active processing clips the gained input at approximately $\pm5$\,V and
converts it to signed 8-bit samples. Negative Output Level or negative level
CV inverts polarity. At zero level the lane is silent. Bypass retains Input
Gain but skips level, filtering and input quantization/clipping; switching
it can therefore change the level substantially.
