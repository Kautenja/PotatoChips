\manualchapter{Start with one wavetable voice}{sec:start}
Name Corp emulates the Namco 163's multiplexed wavetable voices. It exposes
five editable waves, interpolation between them, and up to eight active
oscillators per polyphonic channel. Active voice count changes tuning.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Initialize the module. Set Active Channels to 1 and leave its CV empty.
Only the highest-numbered voice, voice 8, is active in this configuration.
\item Connect output 8 to a mixer at a low level. Keep voice 8 Level at 15,
the global Morph at 1 and FM at zero. Tune with its frequency knob and a tuner.
\item Patch a sequencer's pitch to voice 8 V/OCT. For articulation, patch a
0--10\,V envelope to its Level input.
\item Move Morph toward 2 to blend into the next wave. Drag in one of the
five large waveform editors to replace that wave's samples.
\end{enumerate}

For a thicker sound, raise Active Channels and retune. Voices become active
from 8 downward; increasing the count divides the shared chip's time among
more oscillators and lowers pitch. This is part of the chip behavior, not
additional polyphonic cable channels.
