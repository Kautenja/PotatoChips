\manualchapter{Start with the wave channel}{sec:start}
Formerly Pallet Town Waves System. Existing patches and presets remain compatible.

Pocket APU combines the Game Boy's two pulse voices, wave playback channel
and noise generator. Its five editable waves can be blended by the wave
channel. Each output can also carry a sum of preceding, unpatched outputs.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Initialize the module. Lower Pulse 1, Pulse 2 and Noise Level to zero.
Connect the third (Wave) output to a mixer at a low monitoring level.
\item Leave Wave Level at 3 and patch pitch to the Wave V/OCT input. The
initialized wave is audible without a gate.
\item Move Wave Morph above 1 and toward 5 to hear the bank. Drag a large
wave editor to draw a replacement; module-browser previews are read-only.
\item Patch a 0--10\,V envelope to Wave Level. Its four amplitude settings
make stepped articulation; use an external VCA for a smooth fade.
\end{enumerate}

To hear a full chip mix, restore the pulse/noise levels and monitor the last
output with the first three empty. Keep levels low enough to avoid clipping
the accumulated signal.
