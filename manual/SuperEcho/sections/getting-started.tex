\manualchapter{Start here}{sec:start}
Formerly Super Echo. Existing patches and presets remain compatible.

Echo adds a filtered echo to a stereo signal. Use short settings for
comb filtering and longer settings for distinct repeats. Each polyphonic
channel has its own stereo buffer; the eight FIR coefficients are shared by
that channel's left and right lanes.
\subsection{First patch}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Add an oscillator, a VCA or mixer, Echo and VCV Audio. Select your
output device and start with the monitoring level low.
\item Connect a short gated sound to IN-L, and OUT-L to your mixer or Audio.
Leave IN-R unpatched to copy the left input into the right lane.
\item Leave input gain at 0 dB and bypass off. Set Delay to level 15 (the
hover display says 240 ms), Feedback to 40 and both Mix controls to 64.
\item Leave FIR coefficient 1 at 127 and coefficients 2--8 at zero. A short
note produces a dry sound followed by repeats. At 48 kHz the buffer delay
for level 15 is 160 ms, plus the filter's few samples of latency.
\item Connect OUT-R for stereo and try a negative right Mix for inverted
wet repeats on that side. This is polarity inversion, not stereo panning.
\end{enumerate}
Mix and Feedback both default to zero, so an initialized module sounds dry.
