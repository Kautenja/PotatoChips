\manualchapter{Start here}{sec:start}
Formerly Infinite Stairs. Existing patches and presets remain compatible.

Staircase 2A03 brings the Ricoh 2A03's pulse, triangle and noise voices into
Rack. It runs continuously without a gate. Use the individual outputs for
separate voices, or leave earlier outputs unplugged to mix them into a later
one. The original console's sampled-audio channel is not included.
\subsection{First patch}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Add Staircase 2A03 and VCV Audio. Choose your output device and turn
monitoring down before connecting audio.
\item Connect TONE1 (Pulse 1 Audio in hover help) to Audio's To device 1.
The initial pitch is C4, FM is zero and Pulse 1 Volume is 10.
\item Turn the top-left V/OCT control to change pitch. Turn its PW control
through the four pulse shapes; narrower pulses sound brighter.
\item Patch a sequencer's pitch to the first V/OCT input. For articulated
notes, send the gate to an envelope and its 0--10 V output to the first AMP
input. Alternatively use an external VCA.
\end{enumerate}
The triangle and noise have their own SYNC inputs. Pulse voices have no
phase-reset input; do not patch a gate expecting to start their oscillators.
